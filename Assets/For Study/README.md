# For Study

The original scenes, kept for the record and ready to run again. Scripts, prefabs, boards, and recordings are the original files.

| Scene | Device |
|---|---|
| `Scenes/System Final Scene/Leader/SLSL_P1` to `SLSL_P5` | Headset of the leader, one scene per avatar |
| `Scenes/System Final Scene/Follower/SLSF_P1` to `SLSF_P5` | Headset of the follower, one scene per avatar |
| `Scenes/System Final Scene/Monitor/system monitor Test` | PC, Space = next step |

1. Paste your Normcore app key into `Assets/Normal/Resources/NormcoreAppSettings.asset`.
2. Build one leader scene and one follower scene, each alone in the build list, for Android. Press Play in the monitor scene on the PC.

The All-in-One demo plays the narration recordings from here; otherwise this folder is independent of `For Demo`.
